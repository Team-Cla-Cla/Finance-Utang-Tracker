# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the issue, proposal, or code rather than the person.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Protect other people's privacy and personal financial information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, OAuth tokens, or private financial records
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact options described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
